What is AIVA?

Our mission goes beyond simplifying financial operations — AIVA is designed to deliver a smarter, more personalized, and future-ready financial experience. By combining intelligent insights, automation, and real-time analytics, AIVA becomes a trusted companion in every financial decision.

Design Process

This project brought together UX, UI, and Product teams to develop a seamless and intelligent financial experience for AIVA. Throughout the journey, our collaboration with Business and Growth teams ensured that every design decision aligned with real user needs and long-term product strategy. By combining research-driven insights with AI-focused design, we crafted a solution that is both intuitive and future-ready.
